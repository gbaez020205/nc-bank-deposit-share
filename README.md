# North Carolina Bank Deposit Market Share
## Problem
Do banks with more branches in a North Carolina county hold a
larger share of that county's deposits, and did that relationship
change between 2020 and 2025? This matters to corporate leadership
deciding whether to keep reinvesting in physical branches as
customers shift to mobile banking. Because banks tend to open
branches where deposits are already strong, this analysis shows
whether branches and deposit share move together, not whether
branches cause deposit growth.

## Data
FDIC Summary of Deposits: branch-level deposits for every
FDIC-insured bank, reported each June 30. Public, pulled
from the FDIC BankFind API.
